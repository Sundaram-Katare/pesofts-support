## What is PeSoft?
PeSofts is a state-of-the-art online examination and proctoring platform designed to help schools, universities, and corporations conduct secure, remote, and highly scalable assessments. By combining robust candidate management, dynamic question banks, and automated proctoring services, PeSofts ensures academic integrity with a frictionless user experience.

## Where to find it
To access the administrator controls, navigate to your platform workspace dashboard (typically hosted at `yourtenant.pesofts.com`). If you don't have an active tenant workspace yet, visit the product website to request administration credentials.

## Configuration steps
1. **Log in** to your administrator workspace account.
2. Complete your **organization profile** details under **Platform Settings**.
3. Define your first **Question Bank** to begin compiling assessment resources.
4. Set up an exam and configure scheduling configurations.

## Related settings
- **User Roles & Permissions**: Set administrator, examiner, and candidate privileges.
- **Platform Settings**: Change custom domains, email gateways, and school logos.
